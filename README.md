# ▲ Wind Waker HD Checklist

## What it is

A checklist for The Wind Waker HD (Wii U) that you can use on your phone while you play. Check things off as you go and see your progress on a separate Hub page. I was only able to find full walkthroughs or overall checklists that weren't in order by location. So I vibecoded one. 

## Features

▲ Follows the Zelda Dungeon walkthrough in order, island by island, so you just keep scrolling

▲ Tap an item to check it off, and your progress saves on your phone

▲ Hub page shows your overall percent and progress for each chapter

▲ Tracks hearts, Pieces of Heart, Treasure Charts, Triforce Shards, songs, pearls and bosses

▲ Gear updates as you upgrade. Check off the Sail and the Hub shows Sail. Check off the Swift Sail later and it shows Swift Sail

▲ Back up and restore your progress from the Hub

▲ Add it to your phone's Home Screen and it works offline
