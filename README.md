# ▲ Wind Waker HD Checklist

## What it is

A checklist for The Wind Waker HD (Wii U) that you can use on your phone while you play. Check things off as you go and see your progress on a separate Hub page. I was only able to find full walkthroughs or overall checklists that weren't in order by location. So I vibecoded one.

**Note:** I'm currently testing the flow of the content and looking for bugs by playing through the game using it. It'll take some time, but I will continue to refine along the way.

## Features

▲ Follows the Zelda Dungeon walkthrough in order, island by island, so you just keep scrolling

▲ Tap an item to check it off, and your progress saves on your phone

▲ Optional items are tagged so you can tell them apart from the main path

▲ Chapters menu shows progress for each chapter, jumps you there, and can mark a whole chapter done

▲ Search for any item, press and hold an item to add a note, and review all your notes in one place

▲ Dungeons menu tracks which bosses you've beaten

▲ Hub page shows your overall percent, hearts and magic meter, and a collection card for Pieces of Heart, Heart Containers, Treasure Charts, Triforce Shards, Tingle Statues, songs and pearls. Tap a collectible to see where it is

▲ Gear is grouped by type (bags, weapons, bow and arrows, gear, tools, bottles, key items) and updates as you upgrade. Check off the Sail and the Hub shows Sail. Check off the Swift Sail later and it shows Swift Sail

▲ Back up and restore your progress from the Hub

▲ Add it to your phone's Home Screen and it works

## How to use

### Add it to your Home Screen

1. Open the link in your browser on your mobile device.
2. Open the browser's share or menu button.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen** (some browsers call it **Install app**), then confirm.
4. Open it from the new icon from now on.

Use the icon instead of a browser tab. It keeps your progress safer and works offline after the first load.

### Check things off

On the **Checklist** page, tap any item to check it off. Tap a chapter title to collapse it. Use **Chapters** to jump around or mark a whole chapter done, **Notes** to review your notes (press and hold an item to add one), and the search button to find anything. The round button at the bottom right jumps to your latest checked item or back to the top.

The **Hub** page shows your totals, gear and progress. It updates from what you've checked.

### Back up your progress

Your progress saves on your device automatically. Back it up now and then in case your browser clears its data.

1. Go to the **Hub** page and scroll to **Save & Backup**.
2. Tap **Export file** to save a backup file to your device.
3. Or tap **Copy backup** and paste the text into a notes app or a message to yourself.

### Restore your progress

1. Go to the **Hub** page and scroll to **Save & Backup**.
2. Tap **Import file** and pick your backup file.
3. Or tap **Paste backup**, paste the text, then tap **Restore from pasted text**.
4. Confirm when it asks. This replaces your current progress with the backup.

You can use the same steps to move your progress to a new device.
